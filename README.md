9. Explain the different types of testing (unit, integration, system, and acceptance) and their importance in software quality assurance.
Unit tests- are close to the source of an application. They consist in testing individual methods and functions of the classes, components, or modules used by your software. It ensures that each unit performs its intended fucntion correclty, isolated from other componets.
Integration tests- verify that different modules or services used by your application work well together.
Helps to ensure data flows smoothly between modules and interfaces work as expected.
System tesing- Focus on the entire software system as a whole, including all functionalities and interactions. It helps to verify that the system meets all functional and non-functional requirements, including perfomance, usability and security. 
Acceptance- are formal tests that verify if a system satisfies business requirement. They require the entire application to be running while testing and focus on replication user  behaviours.
Whether the software meet the needs of the end-user and is ready for deploment.
